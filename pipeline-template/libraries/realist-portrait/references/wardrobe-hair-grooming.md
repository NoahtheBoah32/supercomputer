# Wardrobe, Hair & Grooming — paste-ready

Generators default to crisp, brand-new, perfectly-draped clothing and helmet-smooth hair. Real clothes wrinkle, pill, and sit slightly off; real hair has flyaways and an imperfect parting. Naming fabric, fit, condition, and hair imperfection is a cheap, high-impact realism lever.

---

## 1. Wardrobe — always name fabric + fit + condition

**Fabric** (weight and behaviour, not just colour):
`heavyweight brushed cotton` · `fine merino knit, slight pilling at the cuffs` · `crisp poplin with press creases` · `washed linen, deeply rumpled` · `worn denim, faded at the stress points` · `raw silk with visible slub` · `technical shell fabric, matte, slight sheen at the seams` · `boiled wool` · `leather softened and creased with wear`

**Fit** (how it sits on *this* body):
`slightly oversized, dropped shoulder seam sitting below the actual shoulder` · `tailored close through the chest, a clean break at the collar` · `collar sitting a little uneven, one point tucked under` · `sleeves pushed up unevenly to the forearm` · `a gap pulling at the button over the chest`

**Condition**:
`brand-new, still with fold lines` (rare — say it deliberately) · `well-worn, softened, faint staining at the collar` · `freshly pressed but already creasing at the elbow` · `a loose thread at the hem, a missing top button`

**Layers / detail**: name what's at the neckline since that's what a portrait shows — `a plain crew undershirt just visible at the collar`, `a thin gold chain sitting in the hollow of the throat`, `reading glasses pushed up into the hair`.

## 2. Period / register (state one so styling doesn't drift)

`contemporary casual` · `contemporary tailored / corporate` · `workwear / trades` · `1970s` · `1990s` · `period pre-1950 (specify decade)` · `near-future utilitarian` · `formalwear / black tie`

Match jewellery, glasses shape, collar cut, and grooming to the period — a modern undercut on a 1940s portrait is an instant tell.

## 3. Hair — imperfection is mandatory

| Element | Paste-ready phrase |
|---|---|
| Baseline | `hair with natural imperfection — a few flyaways catching the light, the parting not perfectly straight, some strands out of place` |
| Fine / thin | `fine hair, scalp faintly visible at the crown, static lift on a few strands` |
| Thick / coarse | `thick coarse hair with visible individual sections, uneven density at the hairline` |
| Curly / coily | `natural curl pattern with irregular clumping, some frizz halo backlit by the key, defined but not uniform` |
| Tied back | `hair pulled back with loose pieces escaping at the temples and nape, a slight bump where the tie sits` |
| Short / cropped | `short hair with a real cowlick, growth pattern visible at the crown, faint tan line at the hairline` |
| Wet / styled | `hair styled with product, a few comb tracks visible, slightly wet-look at the roots` |
| Greying | `salt-and-pepper, grey concentrated at the temples and through the beard, individual white hairs` |

Backlight note: `key or rim light picking out individual strands at the edge of the hair, fine hair detail against the background`.

## 4. Facial hair (if applicable)

`two-day stubble, uneven along the jaw and under the chin, sparse on the cheeks` · `full beard, groomed but with stray longer hairs, slightly lighter at the moustache` · `clean-shaven with visible shadow and a small missed patch under the jaw` · `moustache with the lip line kept clear`

## 5. Grooming state (match to purpose)

| State | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Polished (brand hero, formal) | `carefully groomed, skin matte from light powder, brows shaped, no stray hairs` |
| Natural (documentary, editorial) | `minimal grooming, skin left with its natural shine, brows unshaped` |
| Dishevelled (character, candid) | `tired and slightly unkempt, hair not fixed, collar askew, faint shine on the face` |
| Working (trades, sport) | `sweat at the hairline and temples, a smudge of dirt on one cheek, dust in the eyebrows` |

## 6. Makeup (if worn — name the finish, not just "makeup")

`natural makeup: skin evened but pores still visible, soft matte lip in a bitten-in tone, mascara only, brows brushed up` · `full editorial makeup: defined eye, sculpted cheek, glossy lip, skin still textured under it` · `no makeup, bare skin, natural lip colour and slight under-eye shadow`

## 7. Anti-patterns

- "Stylish outfit", "fashionable clothing", "elegant dress" — no fabric, no fit, model defaults to glossy catalogue render.
- Perfectly symmetric, glass-smooth hair with a laser parting.
- Every garment box-fresh and wrinkle-free unless you said "new".
- Jewellery that melts into the skin — if there's jewellery, give it `a hard specular highlight and a real cast shadow on the skin`.
