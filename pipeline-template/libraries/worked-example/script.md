PREMISE
Waffles, an indoor cat who has only ever seen the city through one window, is carried across
town in a laundry van and has to find his way home using the three landmarks he stares at every
day — except from the ground, none of them look the way they do from the sill.

EMOTIONAL CORE
Feeling: giddy panic resolving into warm, laughing relief.
Idea: the view you watch every day is the map home.
